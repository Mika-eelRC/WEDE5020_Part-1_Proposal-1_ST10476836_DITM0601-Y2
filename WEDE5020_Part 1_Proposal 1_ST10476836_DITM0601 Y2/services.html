Our Services & Community Programs

Empowering caregivers and community educators to deliver accessible, high-quality early childhood development.

Supporting Every Step of Early Learning

At FCW, we focus on home-based and community-centered solutions. We equip parents, fieldworkers, and local playgroups with practical tools and training so children in under-resourced areas get the best start in life.

1. Family in Focus (Home-Based ECD)

Family in Focus is our primary home-visiting initiative designed for parents and caregivers who do not have access to formal preschools or crèches.

  • Trained community fieldworkers visit households directly to guide parents.
  • Provides age-appropriate play activities using everyday household materials.
  • Fosters parent-child bonding while developing essential early cognitive skills.

2. ECD Practitioner & Fieldworker Training

We build capacity within local communities by offering structured training workshops for informal playgroup facilitators, caregivers, and community workers.

  • Practical workshops on early learning literacy, numeracy, and social play.
  • Health, nutrition, and child safety guidance for early learning groups.
  • Mentorship and ongoing monitoring for local fieldworkers across Western Cape communities.

3. Educational Resource Kits & Toy Libraries

Access to learning materials is vital for early development. FCW assembles and distributes custom educational kits tailored for young minds.

  • Home-Play Kits: Storybooks, puzzles, and drawing materials for low-income households.
  • Community Toy Libraries: Rotating educational toys and sensory materials for informal playgroups.
  • Parenting Guides: Downloadable and printed resource booklets in local languages.
