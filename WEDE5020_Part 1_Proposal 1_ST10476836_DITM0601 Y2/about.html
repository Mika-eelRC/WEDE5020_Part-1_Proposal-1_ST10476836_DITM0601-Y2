About Our Foundation

Empowering communities and strengthening families through accessible early education since 1974.

Our Story & History

The Foundation for Community Work (FCW) is a registered non-profit organisation based in the Western Cape. For decades, FCW has been committed to promoting early childhood development (ECD) in vulnerable and under-resourced communities.

We believe that parents and local caregivers are a child's primary educators. By working directly within local neighbourhoods, we help build supportive environments where young children can reach their full potential.

Our Driving Purpose

Our Mission

To promote holistic early childhood development through home-based programs, community training, and resource empowerment for families.

Our Vision

A society where all children, regardless of background, have access to quality early learning and supportive family care.

Core Values

Community Empowerment, Equality, Integrity, Compassion, and Continuous Learning in early childhood care.
